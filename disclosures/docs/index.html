Disclosures

Where the money is moving — 13F flows across every processed manager, officials' trades and the signals where they line up — plus institutional ownership, public officials' disclosures and market positioning, read from the public record: SEC 13F, 8-K, Form 4, Schedule 13D / 13G, Form 144 and N-PX filings; FINRA short interest; press releases (PR Newswire) and government releases (FDA approvals, SEC enforcement); financial disclosures of the US House and Senate, New York State, California, Texas (imports) and Canada's federal public office holders; CFTC Commitments of Traders and FDIC bank financials.

Data: SEC EDGAR (13F, 8-K, Form 4, Schedules 13D / 13G, Form 144, N-PX), FINRA short interest, PR Newswire, openFDA and the FDA's press releases, SEC press and enforcement releases, the House Clerk's financial-disclosure index, the Senate's electronic financial disclosures (eFD), CFTC Commitments of Traders and the FDIC BankFind API. Collected on a schedule in batches; coverage grows run by run.
For information and research, not investment advice. Report dates differ from publication dates. Historical snapshots are not a complete point-in-time archive.
