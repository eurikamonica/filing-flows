Annual General Meeting

 

Check Point Software Technologies Ltd. (the “Company”) held its Annual General Meeting on September 2, 2026. A total of 81.2 million Ordinary Shares, par value NIS 0.01 per share, of the Company (“Ordinary Shares”) held by shareholders of record at the close of business on July 16, 2026 (the “Record Date”) were present and entitled to vote at the Annual General Meeting.
