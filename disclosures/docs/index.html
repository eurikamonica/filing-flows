Disclosures

Institutional ownership, public officials' disclosures and market positioning, read from the public record: SEC 13F and N-PX filings; financial disclosures of the US House, New York State, California, Texas (imports) and Canada's federal public office holders; CFTC Commitments of Traders and FDIC bank financials.

Data: SEC EDGAR (13F, N-PX), the House Clerk's financial-disclosure index, CFTC Commitments of Traders and the FDIC BankFind API. Collected on a schedule in batches; coverage grows run by run.
For information and research, not investment advice. Report dates differ from publication dates. Historical snapshots are not a complete point-in-time archive.
